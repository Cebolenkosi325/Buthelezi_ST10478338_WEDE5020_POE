About Us

Sunrise Bakery is a family-owned bakery established in 2015. We are passionate about providing fresh and affordable baked goods to our local community.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality, affordable and freshly baked goods using traditional recipes and fresh ingredients.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the town's most loved neighbourhood bakery, known for quality products and friendly service.
